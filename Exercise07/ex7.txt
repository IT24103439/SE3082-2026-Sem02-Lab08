Observation
Implementing Buffered Send (MPI_Bsend) along with MPI_ANY_SOURCE allowed worker processes to immediately place their computed partial counts into attached memory buffers and complete their send operations without waiting for Rank 0 to match them directly.
The program produced accurate approximations of Pi around 3.142 across all process counts.
The execution time scaled down smoothly from about 0.167 seconds on 1 process to 0.021 seconds on 8 processes.
This confirms that buffering outbound data combined with order-independent message receiving keeps processor idle time to a minimum and provides optimal parallel throughput.
