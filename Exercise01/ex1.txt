Observation
When running the program with different numbers of processes (2, 4, and 7), each process correctly identified its unique rank and printed the hostname "LEGION".
Rank 0 always printed the "Hello World" message, while the other ranks printed "Just a normal process".
I also observed that the output lines were printed out of order when using more processes.
For example, with 7 processes, rank 6 printed before rank 0.
This shows that the processes run at the same time independently and the order of printing depends on how the CPU schedules them rather than their rank number.
